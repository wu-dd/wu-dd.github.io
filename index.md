## Research Interests

My research explores two complementary questions around <i>imperfect information</i> in machine learning. First, when imperfection is a <strong>defect</strong>: <i>how can models learn reliably from imperfect supervision?</i> To this end, I work on <strong>weakly supervised learning</strong> across classification, generative models, and LLM post-training. Second, when imperfection is a <strong>design</strong>: <i>how can imperfect signals be deliberately crafted to protect models?</i> This drives my work on <strong>AI safety</strong>, including watermarking, model protection, and robustness — turning imperfect signals from a passive flaw into an active defense mechanism.
